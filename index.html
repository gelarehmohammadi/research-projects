Research overview

Understanding people. Designing intelligent systems.

My research spans human-centred artificial intelligence, affective computing, social signal processing, and human–computer interaction. It combines computational approaches with insights from psychology and human behaviour to develop accessible, socially intelligent technologies, with applications in education, health, wellbeing and beyond.

Associate Professor Gelareh Mohammadi

Emotion Recognition and Computational Models
